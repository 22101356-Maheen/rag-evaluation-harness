RAG RETRIEVAL TEST CORPUS

RETRIEVER
A retriever searches the document collection and returns the most relevant chunks for a user's query.
The retriever does not generate the final answer. Its job is to find useful evidence from the stored documents.
Good retrieval is important because the generation model can only answer well when the correct context is provided.

SEMANTIC SEARCH
Semantic search compares the meaning of the user's query with the meaning of document chunks.
It can find relevant text even when the exact same keywords are not used.

BM25
BM25 is a keyword-based retrieval method.
It gives higher scores to chunks that contain important query terms.

HYBRID RETRIEVAL
Hybrid retrieval combines semantic search and keyword-based retrieval.
It can improve retrieval quality by using both meaning and exact keyword matches.

RERANKING
A reranker takes retrieved candidates and reorders them so that the most relevant evidence appears near the top.

RECALL AT K
Recall at K measures how much of the known relevant evidence appears within the first K retrieved results.

PRECISION AT K
Precision at K measures how many of the first K retrieved chunks are actually relevant.

MRR
Mean Reciprocal Rank rewards systems that place the first relevant result near the top of the ranking.

FAITHFULNESS
Faithfulness checks whether a generated answer is supported by the retrieved context.
